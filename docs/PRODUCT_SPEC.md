# Product specification — validation MVP

## Amazon Clean
Goal: marketplace-style primary product image.
- pure white background request
- improve clarity, exposure and color
- preserve visible product shape/details/branding as closely as possible
- no props, no added text

## Studio
Goal: professional commercial studio image.
Initial preset: Soft Gray Studio.
- neutral light-gray studio background
- professional light
- realistic contact shadow
- preserve product identity

## Lifestyle
Goal: place the same product into a premium context.
Initial preset: Scandinavian Interior.
- natural daylight
- minimal decor
- realistic contact shadow
- product remains focal point

## Feedback taxonomy
On thumbs-down ask for one or more reasons:
- PRODUCT_CHANGED
- WRONG_COLOR
- LOGO_OR_TEXT_CHANGED
- MISSING_DETAIL
- STILL_BLURRY
- BACKGROUND_WRONG
- LOOKS_ARTIFICIAL
- OTHER
